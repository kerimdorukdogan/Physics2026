# 🛰️ Problem 2 — Orbital Mechanics

## 📌 Given

ISS altitude:

h = 400 km

Earth radius:

R = 6378 km

Earth mass:

M = 5.97 × 10^24 kg

Gravitational constant:

G = 6.67 × 10^-11 N·m²/kg²

Earth-Sun distance:

r = 150 × 10^6 km

Earth orbital period:

T = 365.25 days

---

# 🔸 Step 1 — ISS Orbital Radius

r = R + h

r = 6378 + 400

r = 6778 km

r = 6.778 × 10^6 m

---

# 🔸 Step 2 — ISS Orbital Speed

Formula:

v = √(GM/r)

v = √[(6.67 × 10^-11 × 5.97 × 10^24)/(6.778 × 10^6)]

v ≈ 7660 m/s

v ≈ 7.66 km/s

---

# 🔸 Step 3 — Earth's Orbital Speed Around the Sun

Orbit circumference:

C = 2πr

C = 2π(150 × 10^6)

C ≈ 942 × 10^6 km

Orbital period:

T = 365.25 × 24 × 3600

T ≈ 31,557,600 s

Speed:

v = C / T

v ≈ 29.9 km/s

---

# ✅ Final Answer

- 🔹 ISS orbital speed ≈ 7.66 km/s
- 🔹 Earth orbital speed around the Sun ≈ 29.9 km/s
- 🔹 Earth moves faster around the Sun

---

# 🧠 Main Idea

The ISS moves very fast around the Earth, but the Earth travels even faster around the Sun because it must complete a much larger orbit in one year.
