# 🌍 Problem 1 — Rotational Velocity

## 📌 Given

Earth radius:

R = 6378 km

Rotation period:

T = 24 h

T = 24 × 3600 = 86400 s

---

# 🔸 Step 1 — Calculate the Circumference

Formula:

C = 2πR

C = 2 × π × 6378

C ≈ 40074 km

---

# 🔸 Step 2 — Calculate the Linear Speed

Formula:

v = C / T

v = 40074 / 86400

v ≈ 0.464 km/s

---

# ✅ Final Answer

- 🔹 Earth's circumference ≈ 40074 km
- 🔹 Linear speed at the equator ≈ 0.464 km/s

---

# 🧠 Main Idea

A point on the Earth's equator travels one full circumference every 24 hours. Dividing the circumference by the rotation period gives the linear speed due to Earth's rotation.
