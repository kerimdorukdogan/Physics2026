# 🌍 Problem 3 — Microgravity

## 📌 Given

Earth radius:

R = 6378 km

ISS altitude:

h = 400 km

Acceleration due to gravity at Earth's surface:

g₀ = 9.81 m/s²

---

# 🔸 Step 1 — Distance from Earth's Center

r = R + h

r = 6378 + 400

r = 6778 km

---

# 🔸 Step 2 — Gravity at ISS Altitude

Formula:

g = g₀ × (R/r)²

g = 9.81 × (6378/6778)²

g ≈ 8.69 m/s²

---

# 🔸 Step 3 — Explain Weightlessness

Astronauts are not outside gravity.

The gravity at the ISS is still about 8.69 m/s².

The ISS and the astronauts are continuously falling toward the Earth together.

Because they fall at the same rate, astronauts do not feel a support force and experience apparent weightlessness.

---

# ✅ Final Answer

- 🔹 Gravity at ISS altitude ≈ 8.69 m/s²
- 🔹 Astronauts are not weightless because gravity disappears
- 🔹 They experience weightlessness because they are in continuous free fall around the Earth

---

# 🧠 Main Idea

Gravity at the ISS is still strong. Microgravity occurs because the ISS and everything inside it are constantly falling together around the Earth.
