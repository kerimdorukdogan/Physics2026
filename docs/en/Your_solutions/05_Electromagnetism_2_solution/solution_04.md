# 🔄 Magnetic Torque — Solution

## Problem

A rectangular loop (10 cm × 5 cm) carries current 2 A.

Magnetic field B = 0.3 T is parallel to the plane of the loop.

Find the torque.

---

## Given

| Quantity | Value |
|---|---:|
| Length | 0.10 m |
| Width | 0.05 m |
| Current | I = 2 A |
| Field | B = 0.3 T |

---

## Step 1: Area

A = length × width

A = 0.10 × 0.05 = 0.005 m²

---

## Step 2: Formula

Torque:

τ = I A B sinθ

Field is parallel to plane → θ = 90°

sinθ = 1

---

## Step 3: Calculation

τ = 2 × 0.005 × 0.3

τ = 0.003 N·m

---

## Final Answer

**τ = 3.0 × 10⁻³ N·m**
