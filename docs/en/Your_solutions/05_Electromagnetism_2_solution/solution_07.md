# 🌈 Wavelength and Frequency — Solution

## Problem

λ = 550 nm

Find color and frequency

---

## Step 1: Convert

λ = 550 × 10⁻⁹ m

---

## Step 2: Frequency

f = c / λ

f = (3 × 10⁸) / (550 × 10⁻⁹)

f ≈ 5.45 × 10¹⁴ Hz

---

## Step 3: Color

550 nm corresponds to **green light**

---

## Final Answer

Color: Green  
f ≈ 5.45 × 10¹⁴ Hz
