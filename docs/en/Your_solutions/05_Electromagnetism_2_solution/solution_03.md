# 🧲 Biot–Savart Law — Solution

## Problem

A wire segment of length 0.1 m carries a current of 3 A.

It is 0.2 m away from point P and perpendicular to the line connecting it to P.

Find the magnetic field at point P.

---

## Given

| Quantity | Value |
|---|---:|
| Current | I = 3 A |
| Length | l = 0.1 m |
| Distance | r = 0.2 m |
| Constant | μ₀ / 4π = 10⁻⁷ |

---

## Step 1: Formula

B = (μ₀ / 4π) × (I l sinθ) / r²

Since angle = 90° → sinθ = 1

---

## Step 2: Calculation

B = 10⁻⁷ × (3 × 0.1) / (0.2²)

B = 10⁻⁷ × 0.3 / 0.04

B = 7.5 × 10⁻⁷ T

---

## Final Answer

**B = 7.5 × 10⁻⁷ T**
