# 🌐 Gauss's Law — Solution

## Problem

A point charge of **+2 C** is located at the origin.

Calculate the electric flux through a spherical surface of radius **1 m** centered at the origin.

---

## Given

| Quantity | Value |
|---|---:|
| Charge | Q = +2 C |
| Radius | r = 1 m |
| Permittivity of free space | ε₀ = 8.85 × 10⁻¹² C²/(N·m²) |

---

## Step 1: Use Gauss's Law

Electric flux through a closed surface is:

ΦE = Qinside / ε₀

Since the charge is inside the sphere:

Qinside = 2 C

---

## Step 2: Substitute values

ΦE = 2 / (8.85 × 10⁻¹²)

ΦE ≈ 2.26 × 10¹¹ N·m²/C

---

## Final Answer

**ΦE ≈ 2.26 × 10¹¹ N·m²/C**

---

## Key Idea

The total electric flux depends only on the enclosed charge.

The radius of the sphere does not change the total flux.
