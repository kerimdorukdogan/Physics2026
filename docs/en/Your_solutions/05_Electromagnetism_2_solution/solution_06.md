# 🌊 EM Wave Analysis — Solution

## Problem

E(x,t) = 100 sin(10⁷ x − ωt) V/m

Find:
- Direction of propagation
- Wavelength λ
- Angular frequency ω
- Magnetic field equation

---

## Step 1: Direction

Wave form:

sin(kx − ωt)

→ Wave travels in **+x direction**

---

## Step 2: Wavelength

k = 10⁷

λ = 2π / k

λ = 2π / 10⁷

λ ≈ 6.28 × 10⁻⁷ m

---

## Step 3: Angular frequency

ω = k c

c = 3 × 10⁸

ω = 10⁷ × 3 × 10⁸

ω = 3 × 10¹⁵ rad/s

---

## Step 4: Magnetic field

B = E / c

B₀ = 100 / (3 × 10⁸)

B₀ ≈ 3.3 × 10⁻⁷ T

---

## Final Answer

Direction: +x  
λ ≈ 6.28 × 10⁻⁷ m  
ω = 3 × 10¹⁵ rad/s  
B(x,t) = B₀ sin(10⁷x − ωt)
