# ⚡ Problem 1 — Parallel Connection

## 📌 Given
- 🔸 R1 = 15 Ω
- 🔸 R2 = 30 Ω
- 🔸 R3 = 50 Ω
- 🔋 V = 12 V

---

## 📖 Formula

1 / R_total = 1/R1 + 1/R2 + 1/R3

---

## 🧮 Calculation

1 / R_total = 1/15 + 1/30 + 1/50

Using denominator 150:

1 / R_total = 10/150 + 5/150 + 3/150

1 / R_total = 18/150

R_total = 150/18

R_total ≈ 8.33 Ω

---

## ⚡ Current

I = V / R

I = 12 / 8.33

I ≈ 1.44 A

---

## ✅ Final Answer

- 🔹 Equivalent resistance ≈ 8.33 Ω
- 🔹 Current ≈ 1.44 A
