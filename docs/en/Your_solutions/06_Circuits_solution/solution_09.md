# ⚡ Problem 9 — Current

## 📌 Given

- 🔸 Q(t) = 5t² + 5
- 🔸 t = 3 s

We need to find the current at t = 3 s.

---

# 🔸 Step 1 — Current Formula

Current is the derivative of charge:

I(t) = dQ / dt

---

# 🔸 Step 2 — Differentiate Q(t)

Q(t) = 5t² + 5

I(t) = 10t

---

# 🔸 Step 3 — Substitute t = 3

I(3) = 10 × 3

I(3) = 30 A

---

# ✅ Final Answer

- 🔹 Current at t = 3 s is 30 A
