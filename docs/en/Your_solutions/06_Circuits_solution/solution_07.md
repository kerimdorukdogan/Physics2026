# ⚡ Problem 7 — Capacitors in Parallel

## 📌 Given

- 🔸 C1 = 4 μF
- 🔸 C2 = 6 μF
- 🔋 V = 10 V

We need to find:

- total charge stored
- total energy stored

---

# 🔸 Step 1 — Equivalent Capacitance

For capacitors in parallel:

C_total = C1 + C2

---

## 🧮 Calculation

C_total = 4 μF + 6 μF

C_total = 10 μF

Convert to farads:

C_total = 10 × 10^-6 F

---

# 🔸 Step 2 — Total Charge

## 📖 Formula

Q = C × V

---

## 🧮 Calculation

Q = (10 × 10^-6) × 10

Q = 100 × 10^-6 C

Q = 1 × 10^-4 C

Q = 100 μC

---

# 🔸 Step 3 — Total Energy Stored

## 📖 Formula

E = (1/2) × C × V²

---

## 🧮 Calculation

E = (1/2) × (10 × 10^-6) × (10²)

E = 0.5 × 10 × 10^-6 × 100

E = 5 × 10^-4 J

E = 0.0005 J

---

# ✅ Final Answer

- 🔹 Equivalent capacitance = 10 μF
- 🔹 Total charge = 100 μC
- 🔹 Total energy stored = 0.0005 J
