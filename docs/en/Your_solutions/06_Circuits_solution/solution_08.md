# ⚡ Problem 8 — AC Voltage Equation

## 📌 Given

- 🔸 Current: I(t) = 2sin(120πt)
- 🔸 Resistance: R = 50 Ω

We need to find the voltage equation V(t).

---

# 🔸 Step 1 — Use Ohm's Law

V(t) = I(t) × R

---

# 🔸 Step 2 — Substitute Values

V(t) = 2sin(120πt) × 50

V(t) = 100sin(120πt)

---

# ✅ Final Answer

- 🔹 V(t) = 100sin(120πt) V
