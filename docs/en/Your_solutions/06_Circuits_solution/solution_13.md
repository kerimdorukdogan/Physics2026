# ⚡ Problem 13 — Transformer Ratio

## 📌 Given

- 🔸 Primary voltage: Vp = 120 V
- 🔸 Secondary voltage: Vs = 9.0 V
- 🔸 Primary turns: Np = 400

We need to find the number of turns in the secondary coil.

---

# 🔸 Step 1 — Transformer Ratio

Vp / Vs = Np / Ns

---

# 🔸 Step 2 — Substitute Values

120 / 9 = 400 / Ns

---

# 🔸 Step 3 — Solve for Ns

Ns = (9 × 400) / 120

Ns = 3600 / 120

Ns = 30

---

# ✅ Final Answer

- 🔹 Secondary coil must have 30 turns
