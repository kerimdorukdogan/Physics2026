# ⚡ Problem 11 — Power and Energy

## 📌 Given

- 🔸 Resistance: R = 100 Ω
- 🔸 Voltage: V = 50 V
- 🔸 Time: t = 5 minutes

We need to find:

- power dissipated
- energy consumed

---

# 🔸 Step 1 — Power Formula

P = V² / R

---

# 🔸 Step 2 — Calculate Power

P = 50² / 100

P = 2500 / 100

P = 25 W

---

# 🔸 Step 3 — Convert Time

5 minutes = 5 × 60

t = 300 s

---

# 🔸 Step 4 — Energy Formula

E = P × t

---

# 🔸 Step 5 — Calculate Energy

E = 25 × 300

E = 7500 J

---

# ✅ Final Answer

- 🔹 Power = 25 W
- 🔹 Energy consumed = 7500 J
