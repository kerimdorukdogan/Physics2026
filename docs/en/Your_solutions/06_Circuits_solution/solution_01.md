# ⚡ Problem 1 — Series Connection

## 📌 Given
- 🔸 R1 = 15 Ω
- 🔸 R2 = 30 Ω
- 🔸 R3 = 50 Ω
- 🔋 V = 12 V

---

## 📖 Formula

R_total = R1 + R2 + R3

---

## 🧮 Calculation

R_total = 15 + 30 + 50

R_total = 95 Ω

---

## ⚡ Current

I = V / R

I = 12 / 95

I ≈ 0.126 A

---

## ✅ Final Answer

- 🔹 Equivalent resistance = 95 Ω
- 🔹 Current ≈ 0.126 A
