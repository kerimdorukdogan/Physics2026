# 🔌 Lorentz Force on Wire — Solution

## Problem

A straight wire of length 2 m carries a current of 10 A.

Magnetic field: B = 0.5 T

Find the force for different angles.

---

## Step 1: Formula

Force on a current-carrying wire:

F = B I L sinθ

---

## Step 2: Substitute constants

B = 0.5  
I = 10  
L = 2  

So:

F = (0.5)(10)(2) sinθ  
F = 10 sinθ

---

## Step 3: Cases

### a) θ = 90°

sin90° = 1  

F = 10 N

---

### b) θ = 45°

sin45° ≈ 0.707  

F ≈ 10 × 0.707  
F ≈ 7.07 N

---

### c) θ = 0°

sin0° = 0  

F = 0

---

## Final Answers

a) 10 N  
b) 7.07 N  
c) 0 N  

---

## Key Idea

Magnetic force depends on the angle between current and magnetic field.

Maximum at 90°, zero at 0°.
