# 🔋 Energy in a Capacitor – Solution

## 🧾 Problem
Given:

- S = 0.02 m²  
- d = 5 mm = 5 × 10⁻³ m  
- U = 500 V  

---

## ⚙️ Step 1: Capacitance

C = ε₀ S / d  

C = (8.85×10⁻¹² × 0.02) / (5×10⁻³)

C ≈ 3.54 × 10⁻¹¹ F

---

## ⚡ Step 2: Energy

Energy stored:

U = ½ C V²  

U = 0.5 × 3.54×10⁻¹¹ × (500)²  

U ≈ 4.4 × 10⁻⁶ J

---

## 🌐 Step 3: Electric Field

E = V / d  

E = 500 / (5×10⁻³)

E = 1.0 × 10⁵ V/m

---

## 🧲 Step 4: Force

F = (ε₀ S E²) / 2  

F = (8.85×10⁻¹² × 0.02 × (10⁵)²) / 2  

F ≈ 8.85 × 10⁻⁴ N

---

## 🎯 Final Answers

- C ≈ 3.54 × 10⁻¹¹ F  
- Energy ≈ 4.4 × 10⁻⁶ J  
- E = 1.0 × 10⁵ V/m  
- F ≈ 8.85 × 10⁻⁴ N
