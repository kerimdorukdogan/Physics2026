# 🧲 Lorentz Force 

## Problem

A charged particle moves perpendicular to a magnetic field.

Find the magnitude of the magnetic force.

---

## Given

| Quantity | Value |
|---|---:|
| Charge | q = 2 × 10⁻¹⁹ C |
| Velocity | v = 1 × 10⁶ m/s |
| Magnetic field | B = 0.5 T |

---

## Step 1: Formula

Lorentz force:

F = qvB sinθ

Since motion is perpendicular:

θ = 90° → sinθ = 1

---

## Step 2: Substitute

F = (2 × 10⁻¹⁹)(1 × 10⁶)(0.5)

---

## Step 3: Compute

F = (2 × 0.5) × 10⁻¹³

F = 1 × 10⁻¹³ N

---

## Final Answer

**F = 1.0 × 10⁻¹³ N**

---

## Key Idea

Magnetic force is maximum when velocity is perpendicular to the field.
