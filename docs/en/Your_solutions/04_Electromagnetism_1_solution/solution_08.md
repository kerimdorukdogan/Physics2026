# 📡 EM Spectrum – Solution

## 🧾 Problem
Order by increasing wavelength

---

## 🎯 Answer

From shortest to longest wavelength:

1. Gamma rays  
2. X-rays  
3. Ultraviolet  
4. Infrared  
5. Microwaves  
6. Radio waves  

---

## 💬 Explanation

"Wavelength increases while frequency decreases across the spectrum."
