# 🧲 Biot–Savart Law – Solution

## 🧾 Problem
Wire segment:

- Length = 0.1 m  
- Current = 3 A  
- Distance = 0.2 m  

Segment is perpendicular to line to point P.

---

## ⚙️ Step 1: Formula

dB = (μ₀ / 4π) × (I dl sinθ / r²)

---

## 📐 Step 2: Angle

θ = 90° → sinθ = 1

---

## 🧮 Step 3: Compute

dB = (10⁻⁷) × (3 × 0.1) / (0.2²)

dB = (10⁻⁷ × 0.3) / 0.04

dB = 7.5 × 10⁻⁷ T

---

## 🎯 Final Answer

**B ≈ 7.5 × 10⁻⁷ T**

---

## 💬 Explanation

"I used Biot–Savart for a small segment and assumed perpendicular geometry."
