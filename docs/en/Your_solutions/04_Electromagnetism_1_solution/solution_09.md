# 🧲 Vector Lorentz Force — Solution

## Problem

A proton moves with velocity:

v = (2i − 4j + k) m/s

Magnetic field:

B = (i + 2j − k) T

Find the magnitude of the magnetic force.

---

## Step 1: Formula

Magnetic force:

F = q (v × B)

We need the magnitude:

|F| = q |v × B|

---

## Step 2: Cross product

v × B = determinant:

|  i   j   k |
|  2  -4   1 |
|  1   2  -1 |

---

### Compute:

i( (-4)(-1) − (1)(2) )  
− j( (2)(-1) − (1)(1) )  
+ k( (2)(2) − (-4)(1) )

---

### Simplify:

i(4 − 2) = 2i  
− j(-2 − 1) = +3j  
+ k(4 + 4) = 8k  

---

So:

v × B = (2i + 3j + 8k)

---

## Step 3: Magnitude

|v × B| = √(2² + 3² + 8²)

= √(4 + 9 + 64)

= √77 ≈ 8.77

---

## Step 4: Multiply by charge

q = 1.6 × 10⁻¹⁹ C

F = q |v × B|

F ≈ (1.6 × 10⁻¹⁹)(8.77)

F ≈ 1.4 × 10⁻¹⁸ N

---

## Final Answer

**F ≈ 1.4 × 10⁻¹⁸ N**

---

## Key Idea

Magnetic force depends on the cross product, so only the perpendicular components contribute.
