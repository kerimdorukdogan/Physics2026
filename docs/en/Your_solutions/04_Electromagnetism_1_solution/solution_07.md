# 🌀 Cyclotron Motion

## Problem

An electron is accelerated through a potential difference of 5000 V, then enters a magnetic field B = 0.1 T perpendicular to its velocity.

Find the radius of its circular path.

---

## Given

| Quantity | Value |
|---|---:|
| Voltage | V = 5000 V |
| Magnetic field | B = 0.1 T |
| Electron charge | e = 1.6 × 10⁻¹⁹ C |
| Electron mass | m = 9.11 × 10⁻³¹ kg |

---

## Step 1: Find velocity

Energy gained from voltage:

(1/2)mv² = eV

v = sqrt(2eV / m)

---

## Step 2: Substitute

v = sqrt(2 × 1.6×10⁻¹⁹ × 5000 / 9.11×10⁻³¹)

v ≈ sqrt(1.6×10⁻¹⁵ / 9.11×10⁻³¹)

v ≈ sqrt(1.76×10¹⁵)

v ≈ 4.2 × 10⁷ m/s

---

## Step 3: Radius formula

Magnetic force provides centripetal force:

qvB = mv² / r

r = mv / (qB)

---

## Step 4: Substitute

r = (9.11×10⁻³¹ × 4.2×10⁷) / (1.6×10⁻¹⁹ × 0.1)

---

## Step 5: Compute

Numerator:
≈ 3.83 × 10⁻²³

Denominator:
≈ 1.6 × 10⁻²⁰

r ≈ 2.4 × 10⁻³ m

---

## Final Answer

**r ≈ 2.4 mm**

---

## Key Idea

Voltage gives kinetic energy, and magnetic field bends the motion into a circle.
