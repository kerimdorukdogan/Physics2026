# 🌐 Gauss’s Law – Solution

## 🧾 Problem
A point charge of +2 C is at the origin.  
Find the electric flux through a spherical surface (r = 1 m).

---

## ⚙️ Step 1: Gauss’s Law

Φ = Q_enclosed / ε₀

---

## 📊 Step 2: Values

- Q = 2 C  
- ε₀ = 8.85 × 10⁻¹²  

---

## 🧮 Step 3: Compute

Φ = 2 / (8.85 × 10⁻¹²)

Φ ≈ 2.26 × 10¹¹ N·m²/C

---

## 🎯 Final Answer

**Φ ≈ 2.26 × 10¹¹ N·m²/C**

---

## 💬 Explanation

"The flux depends only on the enclosed charge, not the radius of the sphere."
