# 🌊 EM Wave Analysis – Solution

## 🧾 Problem
E(x,t) = 100 sin(10⁷ x − ωt)

---

## 🔁 Step 1: Direction

Form: sin(kx − ωt)  

→ Wave moves in **+x direction**

---

## 📐 Step 2: Wavelength

k = 10⁷  

λ = 2π / k  

λ = 2π / 10⁷  

λ ≈ 6.28 × 10⁻⁷ m

---

## ⚡ Step 3: Angular frequency

ω = kc  

c = 3 × 10⁸  

ω = (10⁷)(3×10⁸)

ω = 3 × 10¹⁵ rad/s

---

## 🧲 Step 4: Magnetic field

B = E / c  

B₀ = 100 / (3×10⁸)

B₀ ≈ 3.3 × 10⁻⁷ T

---

## 🎯 Final Answers

- Direction: +x  
- λ ≈ 6.28 × 10⁻⁷ m  
- ω = 3 × 10¹⁵ rad/s  
- B(x,t) = B₀ sin(10⁷x − ωt)
