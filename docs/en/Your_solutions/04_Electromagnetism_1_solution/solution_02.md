# 🧲 Ampere’s Law – Solution

## 🧾 Problem
Two long wires:

- Distance = 10 cm  
- Current = 5 A (opposite directions)  

Find magnetic field at midpoint.

---

## ⚙️ Step 1: Formula

B = μ₀ I / (2πr)

---

## 📐 Step 2: Distance

Midpoint → r = 0.05 m

---

## 🧠 Step 3: Direction

- Opposite currents → fields **add up**
- Direction: same at midpoint

---

## 🧮 Step 4: Compute one wire

B₁ = (4π×10⁻⁷ × 5) / (2π × 0.05)

B₁ = (20π×10⁻⁷) / (0.1π)

B₁ = 2 × 10⁻⁵ T

---

## 🧮 Step 5: Total field

B_total = 2B₁

B_total = 4 × 10⁻⁵ T

---

## 🎯 Final Answer

**B ≈ 4 × 10⁻⁵ T**

---

## 💬 Explanation

"Because currents are opposite, the magnetic fields reinforce each other at the midpoint."
