# 🔄 Magnetic Torque – Solution

## 🧾 Problem
Rectangular loop:

- 10 cm × 5 cm  
- Current = 2 A  
- B = 0.3 T  

Field is parallel to the plane.

---

## ⚙️ Step 1: Formula

τ = I A B sinθ

---

## 📐 Step 2: Area

A = (0.10)(0.05) = 0.005 m²

---

## 🧠 Step 3: Angle

Field parallel to plane →  
Angle between normal and B = 90°

sin90° = 1

---

## 🧮 Step 4: Compute

τ = (2)(0.005)(0.3)

τ = 0.003 N·m

---

## 🎯 Final Answer

**τ = 3.0 × 10⁻³ N·m**

---

## 💬 Explanation

"Torque is maximum because the magnetic field is parallel to the loop plane."
