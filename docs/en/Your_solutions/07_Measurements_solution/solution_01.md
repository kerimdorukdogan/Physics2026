# ⚡ Problem 1 — Propagation of Error I

## 📌 Given

- 🔸 r = (6.20 ± 0.05) cm

We need to calculate:

- volume of the sphere
- uncertainty in volume

---

# 🔸 Step 1 — Volume Formula

For a sphere:

V = (4/3)πr³

---

# 🔸 Step 2 — Calculate Volume

V = (4/3)π(6.20)³

V ≈ 998.86 cm³

---

# 🔸 Step 3 — Propagation of Uncertainty

For powers:

ΔV / V = 3 × (Δr / r)

---

## 🧮 Calculation

ΔV / V = 3 × (0.05 / 6.20)

ΔV / V ≈ 0.0242

ΔV = 998.86 × 0.0242

ΔV ≈ 24.2 cm³

---

# ✅ Final Answer

- 🔹 V ≈ 999 cm³
- 🔹 Uncertainty ≈ ±24 cm³
