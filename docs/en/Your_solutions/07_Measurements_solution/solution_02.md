# ⚡ Problem 2 — Propagation of Error II

## 📌 Given

- 🔸 L = (15.3 ± 0.1) cm
- 🔸 W = (8.4 ± 0.1) cm

We need to calculate:

- area
- uncertainty

---

# 🔸 Step 1 — Area Formula

A = L × W

---

# 🔸 Step 2 — Calculate Area

A = 15.3 × 8.4

A = 128.52 cm²

---

# 🔸 Step 3 — Relative Uncertainty

For multiplication:

ΔA / A = (ΔL / L) + (ΔW / W)

---

## 🧮 Calculation

ΔA / A = (0.1 / 15.3) + (0.1 / 8.4)

ΔA / A ≈ 0.0184

ΔA = 128.52 × 0.0184

ΔA ≈ 2.36 cm²

---

# ✅ Final Answer

- 🔹 Area ≈ 128.5 cm²
- 🔹 Uncertainty ≈ ±2.4 cm²
