# ⚡ Problem 10 — Light Speed Measurement

## 📌 Given

Microwave frequency:

f = 2.45 GHz

f = 2.45 × 10^9 Hz

Measured distance between two melted spots:

d = 6.1 cm

d = 0.061 m

Accepted speed of light:

c = 300,000,000 m/s

---

# 🔸 Step 1 — Find the Wavelength

The distance between two nearby melted spots is half of the wavelength.

λ = 2d

λ = 2 × 0.061

λ = 0.122 m

---

# 🔸 Step 2 — Calculate the Speed of Light

Formula:

c = f × λ

c = 2.45 × 10^9 × 0.122

c = 2.989 × 10^8 m/s

c = 298,900,000 m/s

---

# 🔸 Step 3 — Calculate Percentage Error

Formula:

Percentage error = |experimental value - accepted value| / accepted value × 100%

Percentage error = |298,900,000 - 300,000,000| / 300,000,000 × 100%

Percentage error ≈ 0.37%

---

# ✅ Final Answer

- 🔹 Wavelength = 0.122 m
- 🔹 Calculated speed of light ≈ 298,900,000 m/s
- 🔹 Accepted speed of light = 300,000,000 m/s
- 🔹 Percentage error ≈ 0.37%

---

# 🧠 Main Idea

The melted spots show where the microwave energy is strongest.  
The distance between two nearby melted spots is half of the wavelength.  
After finding the wavelength, we use the microwave frequency to calculate the speed of light.
