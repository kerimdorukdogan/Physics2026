Mass-Spring Measurements

Experimental determination of spring constant using oscillation period

Objective: Measure the period of a mass-spring oscillator multiple times, calculate statistical properties, determine the spring constant using k = 4π²m/T², and estimate the measurement uncertainty through error propagation.
