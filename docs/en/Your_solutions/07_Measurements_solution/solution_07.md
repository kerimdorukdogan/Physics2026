# ⚡ Problem 7 — Standard Deviation

## 📌 Given Scores

88, 92, 79, 85, 95, 81, 86, 90, 83, 77, 89

Number of students:

N = 11

---

# 🔸 Step 1 — Mean

Mean formula:

x̄ = sum of all scores / N

x̄ = 945 / 11

x̄ ≈ 85.91

---

# 🔸 Step 2 — Standard Deviation

Using sample standard deviation:

σ = sqrt( sum((xi - x̄)²) / (N - 1) )

σ ≈ 5.58

---

# 🔸 Step 3 — Remove Highest and Lowest Scores

Highest score:

95

Lowest score:

77

Remaining scores:

79, 81, 83, 85, 86, 88, 89, 90, 92

---

# 🔸 Step 4 — New Mean

x̄ = 773 / 9

x̄ ≈ 85.89

---

# 🔸 Step 5 — New Standard Deviation

σ ≈ 4.31

---

# ✅ Final Answer

- 🔹 Original mean ≈ 85.91
- 🔹 Original standard deviation ≈ 5.58
- 🔹 New mean after removing highest and lowest ≈ 85.89
- 🔹 New standard deviation ≈ 4.31
