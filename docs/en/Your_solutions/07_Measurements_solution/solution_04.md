# ⚡ Problem 4 — Relative Uncertainty

## 📌 Given

- 🔸 Speed = 60 km/h
- 🔸 Uncertainty = 5%

We need to find the range of actual speed.

---

# 🔸 Step 1 — Calculate Absolute Uncertainty

Δv = 0.05 × 60

Δv = 3 km/h

---

# 🔸 Step 2 — Calculate Range

Minimum speed:

60 - 3 = 57 km/h

Maximum speed:

60 + 3 = 63 km/h

---

# ✅ Final Answer

- 🔹 Speed range = 57 km/h to 63 km/h
