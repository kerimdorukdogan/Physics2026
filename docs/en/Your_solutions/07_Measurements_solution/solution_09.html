Pendulum Gravity Measurement

Experimental Determination of Gravitational Acceleration

Objective: Measure the period of a simple pendulum to experimentally determine the gravitational acceleration using the formula g = 4π²L/T², where L is the pendulum length and T is the mean period. Repeated measurements allow calculation of statistical uncertainty.

    Physics Background:
    The simulator estimates the gravitational acceleration using the period of a simple pendulum. The period of oscillation depends on the pendulum length and local gravitational acceleration according to the formula T = 2π√(L/g). By rearranging, we can solve for g. Repeated measurements are used to calculate statistical uncertainty and evaluate experimental accuracy. The standard deviation shows measurement variability, while uncertainty propagation quantifies how period uncertainty affects the calculated gravity value. Real experimental measurements typically yield g ≈ 9.8 m/s² with uncertainties of 1-5% depending on measurement precision.
